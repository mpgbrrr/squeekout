    How to play

    1. Buy in while the lobby is open. Each lobby stays open at least 5 minutes, and you can leave it for a full refund until he starts. Missed the start? You can still jump on for 2 minutes after he starts running, until the first player gets off. Your time on the wheel counts from when you get on.
    2. Stay on the wheel while he runs. Your share grows with every second you stay on: stay twice as long, earn twice the share. Bail early and you'll usually get back less than you paid in.
    3. Get off before he stops. When the wheel has been still for 3 minutes, the game ends at his last movement. Getting off up to 12 seconds after that still counts. Waiting out the 3 minutes doesn't help: by then you're too late.
    4. Get paid. Everyone who got off in time splits 80% of the pot by weight. The last 10% to get off also share a 20% jackpot. Anyone still on when he stopped is caught and gets nothing.

    Good to know

    • Breaks are where the nerve comes in: getting off during a break only counts if he runs again before 3 minutes pass. While he's running, getting off is always safe.
    • If Squeekchain's feed drops while the hamster may have stopped out of sight, the game is voided and everyone gets their buy-in back.
    • If everyone is caught, the pot rolls into the next game. The next lobby opens as soon as he stops, while the last game's payouts are being finalized.
    • A late entry that lands after entries close (2 minutes after the start, or in the same block as the first player getting off, or later) is refunded: claim it back from the game card.
    • Don't want to watch the whole run? Set Auto-off in the game card (for example 8 min 23 sec) and you'll get off that far into his run, even with the page closed. It's a free signature; the watcher keeps your time private and sends your exit when the moment comes, landing in the next block. It follows the same rules as getting off by hand: if he stopped before your time, you're caught.
    • Joined while he's asleep? Turn on Start alerts in the game card for an alarm the moment he starts running. With notifications allowed, it reaches you even when this page is closed (on iPhone, add the site to your Home Screen first).
    • There's no house fee. Whoever finalizes the payouts earns a small bounty (5 SQK, at most 1% of the pot).
    • This runs on the Sepolia testnet: testnet SQK has no value.
