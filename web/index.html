    How to play

    1. Buy in while the lobby is open. Entries close the moment Squeektoshi starts running. Each lobby stays open at least 5 minutes, and you can leave it for a full refund until he starts.
    2. Stay on the wheel while he runs. The longer you stay, the bigger your share: your weight grows 25% for every 2 minutes you stay on.
    3. Get off before he stops. When the wheel has been still for 3 minutes, the game ends at his last movement. Getting off up to 12 seconds after that still counts. Waiting out the 3 minutes doesn't help: by then you're too late.
    4. Get paid. Everyone who got off in time splits 80% of the pot by weight. The last 10% to get off also share a 20% jackpot. Anyone still on when he stopped is caught and gets nothing.

    Good to know

    • Breaks are where the nerve comes in: getting off during a break only counts if he runs again before 3 minutes pass. While he's running, getting off is always safe.
    • If Squeekchain's feed drops while the hamster may have stopped out of sight, the game is voided and everyone gets their buy-in back.
    • If everyone is caught, the pot rolls into the next game. A new game opens as soon as one finishes.
    • NFT jackpot bonus: the game's Squeek NFTs earn SQK from Squeektoshi's work, and half of what they earn from his run is added to that game's jackpot. Those rewards take a while to arrive, so the bonus is paid about 40 minutes after he stops: jackpot winners claim it on top of their payout.
    • There's no house fee. Whoever finalizes the payouts earns a small bounty (5 SQK, at most 1% of the pot).
    • This runs on the Sepolia testnet: testnet SQK has no value.
