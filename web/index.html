    How to play

    1. 1Free to Play

      Hold a Squeek in your wallet or staked. One entry per wallet.

    2. 2Join the Lobby

      It opens at least 5 min before he can start. Missed it? Jump on up to 2 min after he starts, until someone gets off.

    3. 3Game Begins

      The moment Squeektoshi starts running. Your share grows every second you stay on.

    4. 4Decide When to Bail

      Once the wheel has been still for 3 min, the game ends at his last move and anyone still on is caught. Off within 12 s of that move still counts.

    5. 5Win $SQK

      Half of his run's staking rewards: 80% split by time on, 20% jackpot for the last 10% off.

    6. 6Play while AFK

      Set an Auto-off time, or get an alert the moment he starts.

    More rules
    • The prize pays out about 40 min after he stops, once the rewards arrive; claim it on the game card.
    • The rest of the Squeeks' rewards (half) goes to the developer.
    • Each Squeek can enter only one wallet per game.
    • Getting off during a break only counts if he runs again.
    • Everyone caught, or the feed drops: the prize carries to the next game.
    • Finalizing pays a 5 SQK bounty (max 1% of the prize).
    • Sepolia testnet: SQK here has no value.
